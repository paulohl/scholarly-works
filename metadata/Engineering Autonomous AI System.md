# **Modular Entry Types**

Each timeline item can be retained, removed, expanded, or replaced independently.

**RESEARCH** — original research output  
**PUBLICATION** — journal, magazine, or formal publication  
**BOOK** — authored book or monograph  
**PREPRINT** — repository or pre-publication research  
**COMMISSION** — externally commissioned or invited work  
**EXTERNAL IMPACT** — documented use, citation, extension, or uptake by independent parties  
**RECOGNITION** — institutional or professional recognition  
**PROGRAM** — establishment or consolidation of a broader research program  
**FORTHCOMING** — formally progressing work not yet published

The comprehensive timeline should remain the canonical record.

Purpose-specific versions can then be generated by selecting only the milestones relevant to a particular audience without rewriting or altering the underlying historical record.
